Source: City of Portland BFM, Fall TAO – D7 – DP Data Export (financial and personnel exports), FY 2026-27 Fall Supplemental requested amounts, 3,736 line items across 271 decision packages. Service areas and bureau names follow the FY 2026-27 Adopted Budget (Ad Hoc Reporting Tool).

Measures: Program spending is the net change in expense requirements excluding contingency, reserved for future expenditure, debt service reserves and ending fund balance. Total requirements includes them; it sums to $523,101,221 citywide. General Fund limits amounts and FTE to Fund 100 line items. FTE is the net change in budgeted positions from the personnel export.
