Source: City of Portland BFM, Fall TAO – D7 – DP Data Export (financial and personnel exports), FY 2026-27 Fall Supplemental requested amounts, __LINES__ line items across __NDP__ decision packages. Service areas and bureau names follow the FY 2026-27 Adopted Budget (Ad Hoc Reporting Tool).

Amounts: net change in requirements, including contingency and reserves; citywide it sums to __TOTEXP__. The financial export leaves out personnel costs generated by positions, so the __POSCOST__ in position costs from the personnel export is added to Personnel; with it, every package's resources equal its requirements. General Fund and Non-General Fund limit amounts and FTE to Fund 100 line items or all other funds. FTE is the net change in budgeted positions from the personnel export.

Mock data: the service impact flag and the type tags (Enhance, Reduce, Create, Eliminate, Realign; CSL add, Revenue replacement, New revenue, Contingency or reserve change, Realignment, Balancing) are not in the current export and were assigned for illustration only.
