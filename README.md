# Portland Budget Treemap

An interactive treemap of the City of Portland's FY 2026-27 Adopted Budget.

**Live site:** https://brycelied.github.io/PortlandBudget/

## What it shows

- **Levels:** City (bureaus grouped by service area) → service area → bureau. At the bureau level, switch between a breakdown by program or by fund. Select a program to break it down by sub-program, and select any sub-program or fund to see its funding sources and spending categories.
- **Funds:** All funds, or the General Fund (Fund 100) only.
- **Budget measure:**
  - **Total budget** ($8.55B): the legal budget under Oregon Local Budget Law, including fund-level requirements such as contingency, debt service, transfers, reserves and ending fund balance.
  - **Capital** ($1.19B): spending on Capital Improvement Plan projects.
  - **Operating** ($7.35B): everything else.

## Source

City of Portland Ad Hoc Reporting Tool export, FY 2026-27 Adopted Budget (expense requirements). The data is embedded in `index.html`.

Totals tie to the FY 2026-27 Adopted Budget: Table 1 for all funds ($8,546,060,062), Figure 10 for the General Fund ($1,186,002,037), and Table 10 for the operating/capital split.

Prosper Portland shows only its City-budgeted amount.

# Decision Package Dashboard (mockup)

A mockup of a public decision package (DP) report for the FY 2026-27 Fall Supplemental Budget.

**Live site:** https://brycelied.github.io/PortlandBudget/dp/

## What it shows

- **Drill-down:** citywide → service area → bureau → decision package, with net change bars at each level.
- **Slicers:** service impact (all / service impact / no service impact), decision package type tags, fund (all funds, General Fund or non-General Fund), and text search.
- **Types:** service impact packages are tagged Enhance, Reduce, Create, Eliminate or Realign; packages with no service impact are tagged CSL add, Revenue replacement, New revenue, Contingency or reserve change, Realignment or Balancing.
- **Ledger:** every package with its type, description, service impact and equity statements, a resources-and-requirements table by fund showing how it balances, and position detail (job class, PCN, fund center, FTE, salary, benefits) where the package adds or moves positions.

## Mock data

The service impact flag and type tags are **not yet collected** in BFM. In this mockup they are placeholders assigned in `dp/build/prep.py` from each package's request type, amount and service impact text. Dollar amounts, FTE and package text are real requested figures.

## Source and rebuild

BFM Fall TAO – D7 – DP Data Export (financial and personnel exports): 3,736 line items and 271 decision packages. The financial export leaves out personnel costs generated by positions, so the $7,417,520 in position costs from the personnel export is added to Personnel requirements. With it, requirements total $530,518,741 against $530,518,739 in resources, and every package balances by fund (DP 28589 is off by $2). Service areas come from the FY 2026-27 Adopted Ad Hoc Reporting Tool export.

```
python3 dp/build/prep.py <DP export.xlsx> dp/build/bureau_sa.csv dp.json
python3 dp/build/build.py dp/build/template.html dp.json dp/index.html
```

When bureaus start entering real tags, replace the mock tagging block in `prep.py` with the new BFM fields.
