Total budget is the legal budget under Oregon Local Budget Law: bureau expenses plus fund-level requirements (contingency, debt service, fund transfers, reserves and ending fund balance). It counts internal transactions between funds more than once. Capital is spending on Capital Improvement Plan projects ($1.19B), and Operating is everything else ($7.35B), matching the Adopted Budget's Table 10.
